Why You Should Go To Lagoon


Five Reasons Why You Would Want To Go

  • During September and October, the park hosts Frightmares, featuring numerous haunted houses and spooky entertainment
  • Lagoon boasts unique, cutting-edge roller coasters like Cannibal—which features a 208-foot tower and 116-degree drop—and the newly added interactive ride Primordial.
  • Your admission ticket includes access to a water park featuring slides, waterfalls, and a lazy river, making it great for cooling off in the summer.
  • Unlike many parks, Lagoon allows guests to bring their own coolers, food, and drinks, which saves money and offers a relaxed, customizable experience.
  • The park is over 130 years old and features a century-old carousel, a classic 1921 wooden roller coaster, and a full-scale Pioneer Village that offers a trip back in time.

Lagoon Description

Lagoon is a premier destination for thrill-seekers and families alike because it seamlessly blends cutting-edge excitement with deep historical charm. Located just north of Salt Lake City, the park offers a world-class collection of roller coasters, including the gravity-defying Cannibal and the classic 1921 Wooden Roller Coaster, alongside an immersive "living museum" in Pioneer Village and a massive seasonal water park. Whether you're looking for the adrenaline rush of a vertical drop or a nostalgic afternoon of hand-carved carousels and carnival games, Lagoon provides a unique variety of entertainment that you won't find at more modernized corporate parks.

Great Events

Throughout its season, Lagoon hosts several signature celebrations, most notably its massive Frightmares event in the fall. This seasonal takeover transforms the park with over nine haunted attractions, including immersive walk-throughs like the "Nightwalk," while maintaining "no-scare" zones in Kiddieland for younger guests. During the 4th of July weekend, Lagoon becomes a prime summer destination with its "Stars, Stripes, and Savings" ticket promotions. While the park typically skips its own fireworks display—often resulting in shorter lines for popular rides—it remains a favorite spot for families to enjoy full days of holiday fun.
