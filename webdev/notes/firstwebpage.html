Facts on Neocaridina Shrimp


General Information

Neocaridina shrimp are facinating creatures that come in many colors, shapes, sizes, and personalities. Neocaridina Shrimp are relatively peaceful, calm, and adorable additions to an aqaurium.
Neocaridina Shrimp tend to be very hardy, tolerating a wide range of PH and temperature. They are often found grazing along the aquarium's bottom, alaways searching for food.
There are many types of neocaridina shrimp, so they will be arranged into different categories.

Cherry Shrimp

Cherry shrimp are my personal favorites since they are easy to breed, raise, and come in such a beautiful color. As the name implies, cherry shrimp are cherry like in color. Baby cherry shrimp can grow up in fresh water, and grow at a consistent pace.
Cherry shrimp typically reach a maximum size of 1 to 1.5 inches in length. Females are usually larger and more vividly colored, reaching the upper end of this range.
They can easily live in temperatures between 60-82°F, but I've notived they prefer warmer waters.

Amano Shrimp

Amano shrimp live in freshwater tanks and enjoy grazing on algae and sifting through substrate for food. Although mature amano shrimpcan endure freshwater conditions; if you look to raise baby amano shrimp, they must be in brakish water.
Baby amano shrimp are born as larva, and until mature must live in brakish water. Amano shrimp typically grow to a size of 1.5 to 2.5 inches, making them significantly larger than common cherry shrimp. Females are generally larger and broader, often reaching 2-3 inches.
